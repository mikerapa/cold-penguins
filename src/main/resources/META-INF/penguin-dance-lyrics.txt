Penguin Dance

Waddle to the left, waddle to the right!
Penguins on the ice, dancing through the night!
Flippers in the air, little feet in snow!
Dive beneath the waves, catch a fish and go!
Penguin dance! Honk honk! Here we go!
Grey little chicks in a moonlit glow!
Black and white, or shining yellow too!
Penguin dance! This beat is made for you!

Repeat the final four lines.
