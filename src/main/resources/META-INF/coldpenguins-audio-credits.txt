music/penguin_dance.ogg is an original electronic composition with original penguin-themed lyrics.
The rhythmic vocals were synthesized locally with Piper using the en_US-libritts_r-medium voice (speaker 0).
Voice model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/libritts_r/medium
Source corpus: LibriTTS-R by Yuma Koizumi, Heiga Zen, Shigeki Karita, Yifan Ding, Kohei Yatabe, Nobuyuki Morioka, Michiel Bacchiani, Yu Zhang, Wei Han, and Ankur Bapna (2023).
Dataset: https://www.openslr.org/141/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The vocals are newly synthesized, trimmed, retimed, processed, and mixed with original instrumental music.
Voice model and synthesis engine are not included in this mod.
Instrumental synthesis and mixing were generated for Cold Penguins.
